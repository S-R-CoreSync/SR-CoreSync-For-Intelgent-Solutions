06

Do we fully own the source code and databases (IP Ownership)?

Yes, 100%. Our contracts transfer every right to the source code, designs and databases to your company — no restrictions, no licence fees and no hidden subscriptions. You also receive complete architecture and security documentation, so your team can extend the system independently.

07

How are invoices and taxes handled for companies in the Netherlands and the EU?

We issue official European tax invoices, registered with the Dutch tax authority (Belastingdienst), including the VAT number (btw-nummer) and the KvK number. Companies based in the Netherlands can reclaim 21% VAT; for EU companies the reverse-charge mechanism applies (0% VAT). The amounts are fully deductible as operating costs.

08

How do you guarantee the confidentiality of our data and the security of our systems under European standards (GDPR)?

We work GDPR-compliant and sign a mutual, legally binding non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before any work starts. We apply end-to-end encryption (TLS 1.3 / AES-256), hosting on sovereign European servers with 99.9% availability and daily encrypted backups.

09

We already run existing financial systems — how do you guarantee integration and migration without stopping operations?

We work with parallel simulation migration. API connectors and posting synchronisation are built in an isolated test environment, and every account and flow is verified. The move to production happens only after 100% verification — without a single second of downtime.
