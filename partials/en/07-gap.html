How are invoices and taxes handled for companies in the Netherlands and the EU?

We issue official European tax invoices, registered with the Dutch tax authority (Belastingdienst), including the VAT number (btw-nummer) and the KvK number. Companies based in the Netherlands can reclaim 21% VAT; for EU companies the reverse-charge mechanism applies (0% VAT). The amounts are fully deductible as operating costs.
